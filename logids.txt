We also need to get more consistent with the naming of these things. Channels were already "add" and "remove", but I think "create" would be more suitable for channels, and "delete" more consistent with messages being deleted. And "add" and "remove" sounds more suitable for tiny things that are added to and removed from bigger things, like reactions. For things like roles I'll use "create" and "delete" from now on, as they are really created and deleted and not simply added to or removed from a certain member. We may change "add" and "remove" for channels later and mass-change already set config options too.
